About My Approach

I provide individual psychodynamic psychotherapy to adults (18+) in Oregon and Illinois.

This way of working is grounded in the idea that we don't always know why we act and feel the way we do, and that much of emotional life occurs out of awareness. Our ways of being are informed by past experiences, as well as current environments and context. Psychodynamic therapy explores how these influences shape your inner experience and sense of self.

By looking closely at the unique constellation of your life, we have an opportunity to learn about how your mind works, how you’ve come to be who you are, and how you relate to yourself and others. Together, we can identify patterns that keep you stuck or unfulfilled, and help you find new ways of addressing or accepting the realities of your experience. Therapy is also a place to slow down and make sense of what hasn’t had space elsewhere, and to feel fully seen. Over time, this can help things feel clearer inside, and support a greater sense of relief, stability, and authenticity.

You can expect a confidential, reflective space to show up as yourself and talk about your life. This includes your thoughts and emotions, your relationships, your body, memories, fears, hopes, dreams-- whatever is on your mind, I’m here for it. This work is attentive not only to what you say, but also to what it is like to speak and experience it. At times we may look together at what hasn’t been said or is difficult to see on your own. Your feelings toward me are part of the process, as patterns of relating to others outside of therapy are likely to emerge in our relationship. This can lead to meaningful insight and change as we notice and understand these patterns together in real time.

There is no right way to do therapy, and the direction we go will ultimately be unique to you (and us). I’ll bring my listening, nonjudgmental engagement, curiosity, and care. My style is warm, with a touch of humor and frankness. It is important to me to create an atmosphere where you feel safe and respected, and we will move at a pace that feels right for you.

Who I Work With

People seek therapy for many reasons. Some concerns I work with include:

  • anxiety and depression (persistent worry, self-criticism, low mood, burnout, feeling stuck)
  • trauma, including relational and complex trauma (childhood experiences, family dynamics, abuse and neglect, sexual trauma)
  • self-esteem issues (perfectionism, shame, imposter syndrome, difficulty trusting yourself)
  • body image and relationship with food (body dissatisfaction, binge eating, emotional eating)
  • identity (race, gender, sexuality, familial roles, cultural belonging, questioning and exploration)
  • life transitions (moving, going to/leaving school, career change, pregnancy and postpartum, loss, illness, caregiving)
  • relationship difficulties (dating, conflict, communication, infidelity, intimacy, boundaries)
  • loneliness (difficulty finding community, feeling misunderstood, social anxiety, isolation)
  • or a persistent sense of disconnection, dissatisfaction, or emptiness (feeling lost, lacking purpose, repeating old patterns, wondering if life could feel different)
Maybe something just feels off or hard to put words to. We can talk about that here.

While I treat people from all walks of life, I often work with self-aware young adults coping with the pressures and uncertainties of adulthood. My practice is affirming and welcoming of BIPOC and LGBTQ+ clients. I consider myself particularly attuned to the experiences of those who may feel outside of dominant norms and expectations, or feel caught between different worlds, relationships, or versions of themselves.

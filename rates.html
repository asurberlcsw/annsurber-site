Rates

My fee is $200 for an initial 65 minute intake session, and $180 per 55 minute session.

At this time, I am at capacity for sliding scale clients. Please contact me if you would like to be notified in the future about availability for low-fee therapy.

Insurance

I am in the process of becoming in network with several insurance providers in Oregon, including Regence BCBS, Aetna, and Moda. For those who want to use their insurance to pay for therapy, I can provide a "superbill" receipt for you to submit to your insurance company for out-of-network reimbursement. If you have an insurance plan that I will be in network with, I can work with you at a private pay rate (and offer you a superbill) until I am in network with your plan. Please check with your insurance provider about your specific coverage and reimbursement rates.
